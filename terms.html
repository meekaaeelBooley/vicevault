About ViceVault

ViceVault is a personal application used by its owner to post video clips to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public, and there are no other users or customer accounts.

ViceVault is used only by its owner, only with the owner's own accounts, and in line with the terms of YouTube, TikTok and Google. Content is posted only through the official platform APIs.
